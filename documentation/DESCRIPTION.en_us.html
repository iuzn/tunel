Tunel is a dot-matrix typeface for LED signs, named after Tünel, the Istanbul underground line that has run between Karaköy and Beyoğlu since 1875. It is drawn with a 2-dot stroke and built to read from a distance: metro platforms, departure boards, scrolling banners.

Every glyph starts as a skeleton on a 1-dot grid, and two rules draw it: every dot becomes a 2 by 2 block, and where two dots touch only at a corner the dots between them are lit, so a curve or a diagonal keeps its 2-dot weight. The two axes change only the dot, never the grid: Weight sets the dot size, from thin dots to touching dots, and Roundness sets the dot shape, from square to round.

To contribute, see github.com/iuzn/tunel.
