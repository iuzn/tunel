Skeletons of the face, on a 1-dot grid. parts.py draws each with the same two rules
(every dot a 2 by 2 block, every diagonal step bridged), so no dot ever sticks out. Glyphs
drawn directly on the face grid are in glyphs.txt.

The letters are proportional and built from shared parts:
  stem and bar: one dot wide here, two in the face
  the corner: a dropped corner dot, which the bridge turns into the face's one curve
  bowl: B D P R share the bowl of P (rows 2-5); B stacks it; R adds the leg of K
  leg: K's lower three rows; R's leg is the same three rows
  arch: n h m share "###." over "#..#"; u is the arch upside down; y is u plus g's tail
  round: o b d p q share the bowl ".##." / "#..#"; C S 3 2 5 end on the same turned terminal
  mirror and rotation: W is M upside down, 9 is 6 turned, d q are b p mirrored, Z z N turn onto
  themselves
Widths: I 1, most letters 4, round and diagonal capitals 5 (twice that in the face). Figures are
4 wide, so they stay tabular.

Header: @ <char|U+XXXX|mark.name> <top row>. Rows of the 1-dot band: 0-1 above the capitals,
2-8 capitals and ascenders (baseline under 8), 4-8 x-height, 9-10 descenders.
