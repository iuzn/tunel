The accent marks set above a letter. Everything else is in skeletons.txt.

Marks are drawn on the face's own grid: a stroke is 2 dots, and a slanted mark is one step of
the face's bridged diagonal ("##." / "###" / ".##"), so every dot sits in a lit 2 by 2 square.
The ring is the one exception: a 2-dot ring needs six rows, so it is drawn with a 1-dot stroke.
build.py sets the row: over a capital or an ascender the mark ends one row above the letter;
over an x-height letter it starts on the row of the dot of the i.

The N, drawn on the face grid: its diagonal runs corner to corner, one column every two rows,
so it fits the width of H. Ŋ is the N with the tail of ŋ.

V X Y v follow the N: their diagonals start in the corners and step evenly, one column every
two rows (every three in V, which is taller than it is wide).

The z: its diagonal must run from the end of the top bar to the start of the bottom bar, which
the 1-dot grid cannot do in three rows, so it is drawn here, turning onto itself.
